# Brag Plan: Sentra

## What is this app?
Sentra is a cross-border trade platform for Africa — one place for traders to submit consignment documents and for customs officers to review, approve, and clear them, with a QR code that ties the whole thing together at the border.

## The angle
Don't sell the idea — show the handoff. A trader creates a consignment, uploads documents, and gets a QR code. A customs officer reviews it and approves it. The trader's dashboard updates live. That loop — submit → review → cleared — closing in front of the viewer is the whole pitch. No metaphor needed; the real product already reads like a demo.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Hard cut into the hero's dark teal background with the headline already mid-motion: "Clear customs 40% faster." slams in, bold, white, center-frame. The "40% faster" gets the emphasis — it's the one number in the whole app worth leading with.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Create Consignment modal fills in field-by-field (trader name, document type, description, value), then "Create Consignment" is pressed.
- Cut to Upload Documents: document cards fill in one by one (Import Declaration, Bill of Lading, Packing List), progress bar sweeps to 100%, and a QR code snaps into view as the payoff of the whole upload sequence.
- Cut to the customs officer's side: a consignment card's status pill flips from amber "Pending" to green "Approved" via the status dropdown.
- Snap back to the trader dashboard: the Approval Rate stat ticks to 100%, and the Risk Assessment card slides in with its green left-border accent — the loop closes in the trader's own view.

## Outro / punchline
Landing page's own second line, re-delivered as the closer: "and keep your business moving." — in the same italic serif treatment the real hero uses — over the Sentra wordmark. No CTA button needed; the tagline is doing the job.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: trader hits "Create Consignment" on their dashboard.
Key action: fills the consignment form → uploads documents → gets a QR code.
Result: a customs officer approves it, and the trader's own dashboard reflects the approval live (status badge, approval rate, risk card).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: confident fintech-style product film — real UI, real motion, no jokes, no filler
- Interpretation: Fewer, longer-held scenes. Let each screen breathe for a beat before cutting. Motion should feel deliberate and fast, never cluttered — restraint reads as competence for a customs/logistics product.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#07211d` (dark teal-green hero background)
- Accent: teal-300 (`#5eead4`-range) for glows/highlights, teal-600 for buttons/status
- Text: white on dark hero panels, `#111827`-range gray-900 on light dashboard panels
- Display font: Manrope (semibold/bold headings)
- Accent font: Instrument Serif, italic (used for the hero's second line — reuse for the outro line)
- Strongest visual element: the QR code reveal at the end of the upload flow, and the color-coded status pill (amber Pending → green Approved)

## Share copy (draft)
One platform for traders and customs officers across Africa — submit documents, get a QR code, clear the border. No paperwork chase.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, upbeat business-bed with tasteful UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0s under the hook at moderate volume, holds steady under the middle scenes, gentle fade in the final 1.5s under the outro line
- Music cue guidance: preset cues available (see cues file). Target strong beats at 16.02s/17.02s/18.02s for the approve → dashboard-update handoff in scenes 3-4; ignore earlier cues since scenes 1-2 run on their own pacing before the beat grid's useful window starts mattering.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — restraint fits the polished tone better than reactive motion
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — a soft key/tap sound on the form fields filling in, a satisfying short "ding" when the QR code appears, a light click on the status-dropdown approval
- Audio-coupled moments: form fields filling in (typing ticks), document cards arriving one by one (soft card-drop tick each), QR code reveal (ding), status pill flip to Approved (click + subtle positive chime)
- Restraint rule: no laser/whoosh SFX, no music stutter-cuts, no more than one accent sound per beat

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The claim — 3s
Full-bleed dark hero (`#07211d`) with the same soft radial teal glow as the real hero. "Clear customs 40% faster." slams in centered, white, Manrope bold, and holds. Small uppercase eyebrow pill ("CROSS-BORDER TRADE PLATFORM") sits above it, already settled.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident, immediate — the claim should land before the beat even starts
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: bed starts at 0s, moderate volume
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Create & upload — 6s
Recreate the trader dashboard's Create Consignment modal: fields fill in one by one (Trader Name → Document Type → Description → Value), then the modal swaps to Upload Documents — three document cards arrive in sequence, progress bar sweeps 0→100%, and a QR code snaps into frame as the scene's payoff.
Sequential/interaction: yes — form fields fill in order, then document cards arrive one by one, then QR code reveal
Audio intent: busy but satisfying — each arrival should feel like a small win, building to the QR code
Audio-coupled idea: soft key-tick per field filling in; a light card-drop tick per document card; a short positive "ding" exactly on the QR code appearing
Transition mood: clean hard cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Approved — 5s
Cut to the customs dashboard: a consignment card is on screen with an amber "Pending" status pill. The status dropdown opens, "Approved" is clicked, and the pill flips to green.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the dropdown click and the status flip
Audio intent: a small, satisfying click-then-confirm beat
Audio-coupled idea: click sound on the dropdown selection, paired with a subtle positive chime as the pill turns green
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Closed loop + outro — 5s
Snap to the trader's own dashboard: Approval Rate ticks up to 100%, and the Risk Assessment card slides in with its green left-border accent. Hold, then crossfade to the Sentra wordmark centered on the dark hero background with the line "and keep your business moving." in italic serif beneath it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the stat ticks up, then the risk card slides in
Audio intent: the payoff settling into a calm, confident close
Audio-coupled idea: a soft tick sound as the percentage counts up; music begins its fade under the final wordmark hold
Transition mood: soft crossfade → end
